Three-Layer Defensible Moat

Layer 1: Brand Partnerships

Joe Wicks + Money Saving Expert lock exclusive trust signals. Competitors can build similar products — they cannot buy the same endorsements.

Layer 2: Data Network Effects

Every family that shares calendar, fridge content, and order history makes Fairy Food Mother smarter. Switching costs compound. Deliveroo cannot replicate from scratch.

Layer 3: Group-Level Strategic Asset

Family food data feeds the Prosus Large Commerce Model across the entire portfolio. Attracts group-level investment, not just local product budget. This is JET's contribution to the architecture Prosus is building.

Approve a 4-Week Smoke Test

Sample Segment B. Measure whether the habit loop works.

  • Profile setup completion: The key assumption. Do families spend 2 minutes creating a profile?
  • Push open rate & conversion: vs. standard promotional control
  • Time-to-order: Does removing friction accelerate the purchase?
  • 30-day order frequency change: Are we building habit, or moving deals?

Joe Wicks and MSE partnerships negotiate in parallel. They do not need to be live to prove the habit mechanic.

Decision Gate at 90 Days
